## 📰 TOP BUSINESS NEWS

### Technology
**1. Apple Announces MacBook & iPad Price Hikes — AAPL -6.12%**
Apple raised prices on MacBook Neo, Air, Pro, iMac, and iPad by up to $200, citing AI-fueled demand creating a global memory shortage ("RAMageddon"). ~$265B in market cap wiped. Analyst Gene Munster called the drop an "overreaction" given no iPhone price increase.

**2. Microsoft Raises Xbox & Consumer Prices — MSFT -3.80%**
Following Apple, Microsoft announced Xbox 512GB +$100 and 1TB +$150 effective Aug. 1, also citing memory shortages. Consumer electronics sector broadly under pressure.

**3. Micron Posts Record-Breaking Q3 — MU +17%**
Revenue $41.46B (vs. $9.3B a year ago, quadrupling YoY). EPS $25.11 vs. $20.78 expected. Q4 guidance: $50B ±$1B. AI data center HBM demand is the engine. CEO: "Record Q3 and even stronger Q4 outlook reflect the strategic value of memory in the AI era."

**4. Qualcomm Nearly Doubles Non-Handset Revenue Target — QCOM +9%**
Raising 2029 non-handset revenue target from $22B to $40B, including $15B+ in data center revenue. Meta partnership for Dragonfly C1000 processor (production 2028). Began shipping to a major hyperscaler in May 2026.

### Economics / Macro
**5. PCE Inflation Surprises to the Upside**
The Fed's preferred gauge showed PCE at 4.1% annualized — highest since April 2023. Core PCE at 3.4% in May, up from 3.3% in April. Fed holding rates at 3.50%–3.75%. Raises questions about rate cut timeline.

### M&A
**6. Merck KGaA Acquires Bio-Techne for $11.3B — TECH +21.4%**
$73/share all-cash deal (36% premium). One of Minnesota's largest-ever acquisitions. Strengthens Merck's position in multi-omics, spatial biology, and cell/gene therapy. Expected to close H2 2026.

### Consumer / Meme Stocks
**7. Wendy's Meme Rally Extends — WEN +13% (Week: +32%)**
New CFO Steve Cirulis (former Potbelly exec who worked with current CEO Bob Wright) sparked a retail frenzy. Reddit WallStreetBets making WEN top-trending ticker. Buyout speculation involving Nelson Peltz/Trian Fund adds fuel. Trading halted briefly for volatility.

### Other
