## 📰 TOP BUSINESS NEWS

### Macro
**1. June Jobs Report Comes In Weak — Fed Hold Odds Jump**
Nonfarm payrolls rose just 57,000 in June (vs. ~110–115K consensus, and May was revised down to 129,000). Unemployment ticked down to 4.2%, but only because labor-force participation fell to 61.5%, the lowest since March 2021. Odds of a July 29 Fed hike collapsed; markets now price roughly a 78% chance the Fed holds. This is the single biggest driver of the defensive rotation and bond strength into the holiday.

### Technology / AI Capex
**2. Chip Sell-Off Deepens on AI-Spending-ROI Doubts**
A Citi analyst note questioning whether hyperscalers' AI infrastructure spend is generating adequate returns, plus reports that Meta may lease out spare AI compute capacity (against ~$145B of planned 2026 capex), spooked chip-equipment names. VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) fell as much as 4.5%; Teradyne fell 13–15% and KLA Corp fell 11.5–14% over the slide. Micron (MU) dropped 8.2% on July 1 (still +260% YTD) in a sell-the-news reaction to its own blowout Q3.

**3. Tesla Tumbles Despite Record Deliveries — TSLA -7.49%**
Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2 (vs. ~406K consensus, +25% YoY, a Q2 record), yet the stock had its worst day in nearly a year. The drop reflects a sell-the-news unwind after a 13%+ run into the print, margin worries ahead of the July 22 earnings report, a ~421x P/E, and the looming expiration of federal EV tax credits.

### Index Changes
**4. Alphabet Joins the Dow, Replacing Verizon**
Effective June 29, Alphabet (GOOGL) replaced Verizon (VZ) in the Dow Jones Industrial Average — S&P Dow Jones Indices cited Verizon's shrinking ~0.5% index weight versus Alphabet's much larger ad/cloud/AI footprint. GOOGL rose 4%+ on debut day, and the Dow closed above 52,000 for the first time that same session.

### M&A
**5. Qualcomm to Acquire Modular Inc. for ~$3.92B**
Announced June 24, funded via a private placement of up to 19.2M QCOM shares — an AI-software push that also raised dilution concerns and contributed to QCOM's subsequent weakness alongside soft handset revenue (-13% YoY).

**6. Other Notable Deals:** Bridgepoint → Kayne Anderson Real Estate ($1.39B, June 29); Safran → Exail Technologies (€2.19B, June 27); Adobe → Topaz Labs (AI imaging, June 25).
